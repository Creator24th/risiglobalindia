Capable, reliable, and willing to work to a standard

FAQ

Questions from partners

What does “partner network” mean here?

A group of contractors and suppliers we work with repeatedly on projects, under shared specifications and quality checkpoints. It is not a franchise arrangement.

Is there a joining fee?

No. There is no fee to apply or to be part of the network.

How are projects awarded?

Based on trade, region, capacity and past performance. You will be contacted directly when a suitable project comes up.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within five working days of your application. If we cannot use your services immediately, we will tell you honestly.
