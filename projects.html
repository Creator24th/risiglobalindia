Our approach

What we will publish — and what we won’t

  • Completed projects, with client permission
  • Scope, timeline and outcome for each case study
  • Real photographs of the finished work
  • Honest notes on what was challenging
  • Stock photography presented as our own work
  • Awards or certifications not independently verified
  • Statistics or client names without written permission
  • Project images marked “illustrative” as RGI projects
