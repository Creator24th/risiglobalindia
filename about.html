About RISI Global India

The India arm of the RISI Global group — built to connect disciplined construction practice with international capital, technology and trade.

Who we are

A construction and business-enablement platform

RISI Global India (RGI) is headquartered in West Bengal and serves private developers, landowners, industrial users and international partners. We bring together construction management, real estate advisory, trade facilitation and green building solutions under one roof.

Our purpose is straightforward: make private commercial construction predictable. Instead of renegotiating scope, specification and quality on every site, we apply a documented system — the same way a well-run franchise would, but for construction.

We are at the start of our India journey. Our first pilot project is in planning, and we publish only verified, completed work. Where experience is broader — across Nepal and Japan — we say so clearly.

What we believe

Three principles behind everything we do

▲

Predictable delivery

Buildings should be delivered on time, on budget and to a standard that lasts. Standardisation applies to scope, process and specification — not to quality.

◆

Sustainability by design

Efficiency and durability are design inputs from day one, not add-ons bolted on at the end.

✶

Cross-border by nature

We work across India, Nepal and Japan, and we understand the regulations, suppliers and working cultures on each side.

Our role

What we do — and what we don’t

Clarity matters more than broad claims. Here is exactly where we sit.

What we do

  • Construction management and supervision
  • Real estate and site feasibility advisory
  • Trade facilitation and supplier introductions
  • Green building guidance
  • Market-entry support between India, Nepal and Japan

What we don’t do

  • We are not a RERA-registered promoter or agent
  • We do not offer government or public-sector contracting
  • We do not provide legal, tax or accounting advice
  • We do not publish awards, client names or statistics without verified evidence
