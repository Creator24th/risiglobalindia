Our services

Five connected capabilities. Engage one, or the whole platform.

▲

Construction & Project Management

Who it’s for: Private developers, landowners and industrial users building a commercial or light-industrial facility.

What we do: Planning, site supervision, quality control, cost and schedule management — to a standard specification.

What we don’t do: Government or public-sector contracting.

◆

Real Estate Advisory

Who it’s for: Investors and owners assessing a site or asset.

What we do: Land and site feasibility, development appraisal and asset advisory — advisory only.

What we don’t do: We are not a RERA-registered promoter or agent.

◇

Trade & Agency

Who it’s for: Contractors, industrial users and buyers seeking reliable materials or equipment.

What we do: Strategic sourcing, supplier introductions and trade facilitation along the India–Nepal corridor and beyond.

What we don’t do: Operate as a licensed customs broker.

✦

Green Solutions

Who it’s for: Any client who wants lower operating cost and a more durable building.

What we do: Energy-efficient design guidance, solar-ready buildings, water-wise landscaping and low-carbon material options.

What we don’t do: Certify green ratings — we work with accredited assessors where required.

✶

Global Network & Market Entry

Who it’s for: Japanese, Nepali and international businesses exploring India — and Indian businesses exploring the reverse.

What we do: Structured introductions, market-entry support, partner identification.

What we don’t do: Provide legal, tax or immigration advice.

●

Business Consultancy

Who it’s for: First-time entrants in construction and related trades.

What we do: Project structuring, partner selection and process set-up.

What we don’t do: Act as your statutory auditor or company secretary.
